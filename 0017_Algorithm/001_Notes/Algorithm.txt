ALGORITHMS — PhD LEVEL MASTER SYLLABUS
================================================================

PROGRAM:
PhD First Year — Computer Science

LEVEL:
Advanced Undergraduate → Postgraduate → Research Level

OBJECTIVES:
1. Master mathematical foundations of algorithms.
2. Understand algorithm design and rigorous analysis.
3. Develop proofs of correctness and complexity.
4. Study advanced algorithmic techniques.
5. Understand computational complexity theory.
6. Read and analyze research papers.
7. Develop original algorithmic solutions.
8. Prepare for GATE-level and research-level examinations.

RECOMMENDED LANGUAGES:
C++ / Python / Java
