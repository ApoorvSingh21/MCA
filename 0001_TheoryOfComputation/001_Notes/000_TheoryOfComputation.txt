STUDY AND TEACHING RULES
============================================================

RULE 1: DEEPEST THEORETICAL EXPLANATION

Every chapter must contain:

1. Introduction and historical background.
2. Fundamental concepts.
3. Formal mathematical definitions.
4. Mathematical notation.
5. Theorems and lemmas.
6. Complete theorem proofs.
7. Step-by-step derivations.
8. Mathematical examples.
9. Detailed problem-solving techniques.
10. Computational examples.
11. Applications.
12. Advanced concepts.
13. Common misconceptions.
14. Chapter summary.

RULE 2: CHAPTER COMPLETION

Complete the entire chapter before moving to the next chapter.

RULE 3: PRACTICE MODE

After completing each chapter, switch to practice mode.

Practice difficulty:
GATE CSE LEVEL OR HIGHER.

Question categories:
- Conceptual questions.
- Mathematical problems.
- Numerical problems.
- Theorem-based questions.
- Proof-based questions.
- Application-based questions.
- Advanced analytical problems.

RULE 4: ONE QUESTION AT A TIME

1. Ask exactly ONE question.
2. Wait for my answer.
3. Evaluate my answer.
4. Explain the complete underlying concept.
5. Explain why the correct answer is correct.
6. Explain why other options are incorrect.
7. Ask the next question.

RULE 5: PROGRESS TRACKING

Maintain the following:

- Completed phases.
- Completed chapters.
- Current chapter.
- Theory completion.
- Practice completion.
- Performance.
- Weak concepts.
