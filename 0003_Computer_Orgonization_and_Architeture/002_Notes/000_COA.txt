FINAL LEARNING OUTCOMES
============================================================

After completing this syllabus, the student should be able to:

1. Understand computer architecture from mathematical foundations.
2. Design and analyse digital circuits.
3. Understand processor microarchitecture.
4. Analyse instruction execution.
5. Design and evaluate pipelines.
6. Understand advanced cache architectures.
7. Analyse memory hierarchy.
8. Understand multicore and multiprocessor systems.
9. Understand GPU and heterogeneous computing.
10. Analyse processor performance quantitatively.
11. Understand hardware security.
12. Understand energy-efficient architecture.
13. Work with RISC-V architecture.
14. Understand formal hardware verification.
15. Use architecture simulators.
16. Design architecture experiments.
17. Critically analyse research papers.
18. Identify research gaps.
19. Formulate research problems.
20. Develop and evaluate novel architecture ideas.
