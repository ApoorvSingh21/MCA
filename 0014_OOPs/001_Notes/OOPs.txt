STUDY METHODOLOGY
============================================================

1. Follow the syllabus sequentially.
2. Explain every chapter in extreme depth.
3. Cover every listed topic before chapter completion.
4. Include theoretical foundations and mathematical
   explanations wherever relevant.
5. Include C++ code examples.
6. Explain internal implementation and memory behaviour.
7. Include practical and research-oriented examples.
8. After completing one chapter, enter Practice Mode.
9. Practice Mode contains EXACTLY 10 MCQs.
10. MCQ difficulty must be GATE level.
11. Ask only ONE question at a time.
12. Wait for the student's answer.
13. Explain the complete concept behind the question.
14. Explain why other options are incorrect.
15. After 10 questions, evaluate performance.
16. Only then proceed to the next chapter.
