===============================================================

DevOps Fundamentals:
Understand DevOps culture, lifecycle, CI, CD and collaboration.

Linux:
Master commands, files, permissions, processes, services and SSH.

Networking:
Learn IP addressing, DNS, TCP/UDP, HTTP/HTTPS, ports and firewalls.

Git:
Understand commits, branches, merges, rebasing and collaboration.

Bash & Automation:
Automate repetitive tasks with scripts, conditions, loops and functions.

Build & Deployment:
Understand build artifacts, dependencies, application servers and proxies.

CI/CD:
Automate builds, testing, security checks and deployments.

Docker:
Understand images, containers, Dockerfiles, volumes and networking.

Infrastructure as Code:
Provision and manage infrastructure using Terraform and Ansible.

Cloud:
Understand cloud services, AWS, IAM, VPC, EC2, S3 and high availability.

Kubernetes:
Learn Pods, Deployments, Services, networking, storage and scaling.

DevSecOps:
Integrate security scanning, secrets management and access control.

Observability:
Use metrics, logs, traces, Prometheus, Grafana and OpenTelemetry.

Reliability:
Understand SLI, SLO, SLA, error budgets, disaster recovery and SRE.

GitOps:
Manage declarative infrastructure and deployments through Git.

Production Engineering:
Design scalable systems, troubleshoot failures and manage incidents.

Projects & Careers:
Build production-style projects, prepare a portfolio, and practice
technical interviews and resume preparation.
