After completing this syllabus, the student should be capable of:

1. Understanding the theoretical foundations of Artificial Intelligence.
2. Deriving mathematical formulations of AI algorithms.
3. Analysing computational complexity.
4. Understanding statistical learning theory.
5. Designing and implementing machine learning algorithms.
6. Developing advanced deep learning architectures.
7. Understanding transformer and LLM internals.
8. Designing generative AI systems.
9. Developing reinforcement learning algorithms.
10. Researching causal and explainable AI.
11. Designing autonomous and multi-agent systems.
12. Evaluating AI models scientifically.
13. Reading and critically analysing research papers.
14. Identifying unsolved research problems.
15. Reproducing published research.
16. Designing novel algorithms and architectures.
17. Conducting statistically valid experiments.
18. Writing research publications.
19. Developing an original PhD research contribution.
20. Preparing and defending a doctoral thesis.
